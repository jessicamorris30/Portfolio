About Me

The resume version of me fits on one page. This is everything that doesn't.

I'm a third year Computer Science student at Guelph, and just finished my first Co-op term in IT. I currently have an area of emphasis and interest in cyber security. That's the part of me that shows up in a job application. Away from a keyboard I like to be behind a camera, at the gym, watching F1, or indulging in the fashion world.

These are all hobbies I enjoy and partake in quite freqently. I have an interest in cyber security through its technicality but if I could incorperate any of these interests into projects/a future I'd be more than pleased.

Fashion

It's one of my creative outlets. I love keeping up to date on trends and making them my own. Expressing myself through my wardrobe is extremely gratifying and I love being able to experiment. As the years go on, my love for fashion grows stronger and I gain a better understanding of myself as well. Being able to present myself in a way I feel looks good makes me feel good and confident to take on the day.

Formula 1

I'm not a very sporty person but I did take an interest to motor sport. Particularily F1. Its something I've been exposed to for a long time since its also something my dad and older brother love. I find it so interesting how strategy, design, teamwork, ingenuity, and skill all tie together in such a grand event. A winning team must be outstanding in all five traits to have a shot at the championship and I find it thrilling to watch and learn about how the cars and teams operate.

Gaming

Gaming is ulitmately where I gained my interest for computer science. I loved playing on my brothers playstation when I was little. My Little Big Planet was my favourite game till I switched to other platforms and fell in love with Minecraft. The endless possibilities within the game is what got me so hooked and drove me to explore other ways to create and invent through coding. It didnt stop at coding however I also researched and build my own PC from my love for tech found through gaming.

The Gym

For someone that isnt a particular sports enjoyer its also important to get your body moving and disconnect from the screens. Going to the gym is a recent habit I've built and is a great mental outlet. Building a stronger body means a stronger mind. I try to go four times a week either by myself or share the time with friends too. I'm not shy to group classes either, I particularily enjoy taking pilates and yoga classes in addition to my usual weight training.

Photography

Photography is also a hobby I've had for a while. It started with my sister asking for photos and grew from there. She would post the pictures I helped take and they got some traction. In highschool I then took some photography courses and even won an academic award for my work. Lately I havn't been able to dedicate as much time to it as I would like but its a strong interest none the less. My favourite form is fashion photography but I have dabbled in other forms in my courses.
